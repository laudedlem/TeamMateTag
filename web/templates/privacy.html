Privacy Policy

Last updated May 11, 2026

TeamMateTag stores only the information needed to run accounts, matchmaking, friends, leaderboards, and game statistics. We collect usernames, email addresses when provided, password authentication data, guest and account identifiers, gameplay results, friend relationships, and basic client-side identifiers needed to keep your profile on a device.

We use this information to let you log in, match into games, save your statistics across browsers and devices, support friend challenges, and maintain fair play. We do not sell personal information. Our application is hosted on Vercel and our database is hosted on Supabase, which process data on our behalf as service providers.

Gameplay data may include lineup history, win-loss results, ELO, team-year strikeout statistics, and usage counts for players and links. This data helps operate the game, support leaderboards, and improve balancing.

If you create an account, you can request deletion of your account and associated profile data from within the app. You can also contact us at {{ support_email }} for privacy questions, correction requests, or deletion help.

TeamMateTag is intended for users age 13 and older. If you believe a child has provided personal information to TeamMateTag, contact us and we will review and remove it.
